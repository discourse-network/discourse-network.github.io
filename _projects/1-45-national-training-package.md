This growth project builds on a pilot [Agile leadership training for dRTPs](https://discourse-network.github.io/projects/1-36-integrated-pilot/), currently being delivered across multiple institutions. The pilot has generated strong demand, with 73 applications across two recruitment rounds, with registrations still open at the time.

While initially designed within [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) and [N8](https://www.n8research.org.uk/) to support a structured cross-institutional pilot, engagement has extended beyond these networks, demonstrating broader relevance across the UK dRTP community.

- **Round 1:** focuses on workshops using existing Agile training materials and evaluating participant experience. This is producing a validated high-level training outline (module sequence, topics, and learning progression), rather than a full training package. This outline is being shared with DRIFT to inform refinement of their Agile methodologies module.

- **Round 2:** focuses on transforming this validated outline into a complete, implementation-ready Carpentries-style (modular, lesson-based design principles) training package. This includes developing full teaching materials, structured session content, and facilitator guidance aligned to the outline, enabling independent delivery across institutions.

A key output is organiser-facing implementation guidance, including facilitation notes and delivery structures. The resulting open-access package will support scalable Agile leadership development for dRTPs, reduce duplication across institutions, and enable national reuse of a fully developed training package. This aligns with DisCouRSE priorities for open, sustainable training infrastructure.
