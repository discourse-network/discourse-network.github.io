The Digital Methods Network brings together digital Research Technical Professionals (dRTPs), digital methodologists, designers and researchers to explore how research methods can be translated into sustainable, open and reproducible digital tools.

The network addresses a common challenge in research software: tool conceptualisation and tool engineering are often treated as separate activities. This can limit opportunities for dRTPs, including Research Software Engineers (RSEs), and digital methodologists to work together to shape research tools from the outset.

Through practical collaboration, the network will develop and explore two research tools:

- **ImgNetMaker** – developing the existing prototype into a robust beta tool, with improved documentation, usability and open-source readiness, supporting network analysis of visual collections.

- **AIsearchTracer** – scoping a new prototype infrastructure for capturing AI-generated outputs, including text, screenshots, links and metadata, to support reproducible research into how AI systems surface and rank web-based knowledge.

The project will combine hands-on tool development, partner engagement and collaborative method-engineering activities, including a workshop and an open hybrid roundtable. By bringing together technical and methodological expertise, the Digital Methods Network will strengthen dRTP leadership in method-driven engineering, create reusable research tools, and establish a foundation for continued interdisciplinary collaboration across digital research.
