   As research is becoming more digital, how are the skills and knowledge expected of PRISMs changing? The 4th PRISM Network conference focuses on upskilling members to be ready for managing research investments with a strong digital component.

Through a combination of talks and interactive sessions, we will explore key concepts and the digital research infrastructure landscape in the UK. 

We will also explore how digital research is different from the usual when it comes to leading as a PRISM, managing big data, publishing digital outputs, and thinking about sustainability. 

The conference will provide an opportunity to build practical knowledge, share experiences and strengthen the PRISM community’s readiness to navigate an increasingly digital research environment. Whether you are excited or reluctant to use digital tools and engage with digital research, come to the conference to build your network and position yourself for your next role. 

The conference is hybrid, with attendance open to all. PRISM Network members will have priority for in-person attendance.  [Join the network for free](https://www.pris-managers.ac.uk/join).

Thanks to support from the DisCouRSE network, in-person attendance is free.

A small number of bursaries are also available to support attendance. PRISMs can apply for up to £200 per person towards travel, accommodation, subsistence, or disability and care-related costs. If you require this support to attend, please indicate this on the registration form and the team will be in touch.
